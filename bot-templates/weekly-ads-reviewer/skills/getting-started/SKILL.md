# Getting started: Weekly Google Ads Reviewer

1. Say what the bot does: "Every Monday I tell you how your ads did last week,
   whether you are on budget, and what needs a look. I never change your account."
2. Run `account-setup`, focused on monthly budgets and any planned changes.
3. Ask how they want the review: here in chat, email, Slack, or Notion.
4. Each Monday (routine), ask for last week's and the prior week's Campaigns
   exports plus month to date, or pull them if the API path is set up.
5. Run `csv-weekly-review`. Lead with tracking or pacing problems.
