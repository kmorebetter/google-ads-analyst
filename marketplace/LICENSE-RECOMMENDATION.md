# License decision

Decided: **Apache-2.0**. The plugin is free and open source, listed on the
official Cursor marketplace. Paid extras are sold separately on Gumroad (see
GUMROAD-OFFER.md).

Why Apache-2.0: it is permissive like MIT, adds an explicit patent grant, and
keeps attribution through the NOTICE file. The official marketplace requires
open-source plugins, so this choice makes the listing possible.
