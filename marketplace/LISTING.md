## Name
Google Ads Analyst

## Short description (under 150 characters)
Find wasted ad spend, audit your Google Ads account, and get a weekly review. Works from a CSV file. Never changes your account.

## Long description
Google Ads Analyst helps you see where your ad money goes.

Upload a report from Google Ads and the agent does the careful work for you:

- **Finds wasted searches.** It lists searches that cost money and never paid
  back, and gives you a file you can upload as negative keywords.
- **Checks your account.** It looks for weak ads, missing sitelinks and
  callouts, and campaigns that spend without results.
- **Writes a weekly review.** It tells you if you are on budget, what moved
  this week, and what needs a look.

Every number in a report comes from your own data. When the data is too thin
to judge, it says so instead of guessing. It never changes your ads, bids, or
budgets. You stay in control.

No setup is needed to start. Download a CSV file from Google Ads and upload
it. If you want more, you can connect the Google Ads API later.

## What you get
- 22 skills, including account setup, negative keywords, audit, and weekly review
- 3 checker agents that fact-check a report before you see it
- A plain-language setup guide

## Who it is for
Small business owners, marketers, and freelance ad managers who run Google Ads.
